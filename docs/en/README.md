# English reading copies of the Chinese documents

These files are **reading copies** for readers who do not read Chinese (for example advisors and reviewers). The Chinese originals are not changed and **govern**; in case of any difference the original governs. A copy does not change, add or upgrade any number, conclusion, rule or date. Each copy starts with a header that names its original, the SHA-256 of that original, and the commit of the state that was translated (aa254a3).

Not translated here: everything under `docs/prereg/` (frozen; its protocol and analysis specification already have English reading copies in [../translations/](../translations/)), `docs/PREREG_RESULTS.md` and the root `README.md` (already English). Terminology, retained literals, source passages that are contradictory or outdated (reported, not corrected), and the checks that were performed are in [TRANSLATION_NOTES.md](TRANSLATION_NOTES.md).
